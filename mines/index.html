Company Overview

KMP Industrial Solution Resource Limited is a premier dynamic engineering and engineering-aligned general contracting firm, incorporated under the Companies and Allied Matters Act of 2020 of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.

We specialise in mineral exploration, extraction, and processing — working closely with local communities and government bodies to deliver sustainable outcomes across every project we undertake.

Our Process

Contact any of our stated contractors and negotiations will take place directly.

01

Explore & Permit

Geological survey and licensing with the Ministry of Solid Minerals Development before any ground is broken.

02

Extract

Trained crews work open-pit and shaft sites under strict safety protocols and daily inspection.

03

Process

Minerals are washed and processed on site, cutting transport loads and creating local processing jobs.

04

Export & Supply

Minerals move to domestic industry and export markets with full chain-of-custody documentation.
